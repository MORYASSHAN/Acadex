About the Platform

Acadex was built to solve the complex administrative overhead faced by modern colleges. By consolidating enrollment, subscription management, and role-based access into a unified ecosystem, we allow educators to focus on what matters most: educating.

Whether you are a standalone college or part of a larger university system, our scalable infrastructure adapts to your specific requirements. The platform provides focused access for administrators, faculty, students, and system owners to ensure complete separation of concerns and operational efficiency.

SUPPORT
Frequently Asked Questions
What is Acadex?
Acadex is a comprehensive platform designed for colleges to easily manage subscriptions, students, and courses in a streamlined environment.
How do I subscribe my college?
You can visit the Plans section, choose the tier that fits your college size and requirements, and submit a plan request. Our administration team will review and approve your request shortly.
Can I manage different roles like teachers and students?
Yes, our platform provides robust role-based access control out of the box. Administrators can invite teachers and students, configure permissions, and monitor activity across the college.
Do you offer customer support for new colleges?
Absolutely! We provide dedicated support channels for all registered colleges. Higher tier plans also include priority support with dedicated account managers.
Is the platform secure and compliant?
Security is our top priority. We employ industry-standard encryption, regular security audits, and comply with standard educational data privacy regulations to ensure your college's data remains safe.

Back to Home

LEGAL

Privacy Policy

This page explains the general privacy posture for Acadex. It is intended to help institutes understand how platform data is handled across onboarding, operations, billing, and support.

What we collect

We collect institute account details, administrator profile information, operational platform data, billing records, and support interactions required to provide the Acadex service.

How we use data

Your information is used to operate the workspace, authenticate users, provision plans, improve reliability, and support your institute through onboarding and product operations.

Data protection

Access is controlled through role-based permissions, monitored systems, and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate for education software workflows.

Your controls

Institutes may request account updates, data exports, or account closure through the Acadex support and contact channels. Legal and billing retention obligations may still apply.
